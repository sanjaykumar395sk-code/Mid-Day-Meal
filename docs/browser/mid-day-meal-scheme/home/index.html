About Mid-Day Meal Scheme

The Mid-Day Meal Scheme is a flagship program of the Government of India aimed at enhancing enrollment, reducing absenteeism, and improving nutritional levels among school children.

  • ✔ Nutrition: Improving health and nutrition
  • ✔ Education: Supporting attendance
  • ✔ Equality: Promoting social harmony
